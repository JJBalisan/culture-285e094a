Un-Royal Variety and Loxwood tickets

This is Culture, a guide to the highlights of the week in London. It is a thinner week than usual for confirmed detail, so I will flag where prices are still to be checked.

The three best picks. First, Jonny Woo's Un-Royal Variety at Soho Theatre Walthamstow, from Thursday the twenty-sixth to Saturday the twenty-eighth of November, with David Hoyle, Lynks, Bourgeois and Maurice, Diane Chorley and more. Second, Loxwood Joust twenty twenty-seven is on sale, with dates announced and the first early bird passes nearly gone. And third, Peace Doves, thousands of handcrafted paper birds hanging in the Painted Hall in Greenwich, which runs until the seventh of February.

Queer events

The standout is Jonny Woo's Un-Royal Variety, at Soho Theatre Walthamstow, on Hoe Street in E seventeen, from Thursday the twenty-sixth to Saturday the twenty-eighth of November. It promises live music, cabaret, comedy and queer chaos. The billing includes Bourgeois and Maurice, David Hoyle, Diane Chorley, Jayde Adams, Josh Glanc, Kemah Bob, Kitty Bang Bang, Lynks and Rozalla, plus after-parties. It is queer-led and all are welcome. I could not confirm ticket prices, so check the theatre's site. Thursday has the best availability.

Also this weekend, an extra afternoon show from Robert White, the musical comedian behind That Gay, Autistic, Keyboard, Tanktop Comedy Guy. It is at the Top Secret Comedy Club, on Kingsway, at three p m on Sunday the twenty-second of November. The evening show sold out, and there are limited tickets at twenty pounds.

Further ahead, Taylor Mac brings Holiday Sauce to Soho Theatre Walthamstow, from Thursday the seventeenth to Wednesday the twenty-third of December. It is the UK premiere of the New York performer's Christmas show, with a live band. Prices were not confirmed.

One-offs

Peace Doves is at the Painted Hall in the Old Royal Naval College, Greenwich, until the seventh of February twenty twenty-seven. Thousands of handcrafted paper birds are hung through the Baroque hall. Prices were not given.

Club NVRLND is an immersive, two-thousands pop-party take on Peter Pan, written by Jack Holden and starring Diana Vickers. It is at The Vaults on Leake Street, with previews from Thursday the nineteenth to Wednesday the twenty-fifth of November. Drinks are part of the format, and tickets are through Central Tickets, where the booking fee is not shown up front.

At the National Gallery, Van Eyck: The Portraits opens on Saturday the twenty-first of November and runs to the eleventh of April, bringing all the portraits together for the first time. And at the Quentin Blake Centre for Illustration in Clerkenwell, a Tove Jansson exhibition of original works runs from the twenty-first of November to the ninth of May.

For music, the week's listings include Charlie Dark at Giant Steps on the twenty-second, and anaiis with Tayo Rapoport at the Barbican on the same day. On Friday the twenty-seventh there is a James Brown Live in Paris nineteen sixty-seven night with D J Fraggle at Coldharbour Blue in Brixton. Prices and times were not checked on the booking pages. The E F G London Jazz Festival wraps up on the twenty-second.

Date ideas

Three easy ones. Peace Doves at the Painted Hall is a gentle daytime outing. Van Eyck at the National Gallery is worth booking as a timed slot, and Soho is right there for a bite afterwards. And on Monday the thirtieth of November, there is an Erykah Badu album listening at Shai Space, playing New Amerykah Part One, a low-key evening out.

On sale soon

The big one is Loxwood Joust twenty twenty-seven, at the Kingdom of Loxwood near Billingshurst. The dates are announced: the seventh and eighth, the fourteenth and fifteenth, and the twenty-first and twenty-second of August. Early bird passes are on sale, but only fifty are left for Saturday the seventh of August, after which advanced pricing starts at thirty-seven pounds, and the following Saturday is close behind. There is a new Loxwood Games storyline, the Fortress Dwellers' first UK show, and new workshops. The tickets page lists a grand banquet as an add-on, but I could not confirm whether banquet sittings are open yet, so check soon, because they sell out every year. Catering across the stalls and the banquet is vegan, and there are direct trains from London Victoria to Billingshurst.

In the West End, The Greatest Showman is announced for twenty twenty-seven, but sources disagree on whether general sale began on the eighteenth, so check the official site. The Legend of Georgia McBride, a queer-led musical comedy, plays at sohoplace from the fourth of February to the fifteenth of May, and is on sale now. And Little Dancer, a new musical, opens at the Theatre Royal Haymarket from the fifteenth of February, also on sale now.

Smaller theatres

At Soho Theatre Upstairs, Pink Rabbit, Farah Ashraf's award-winning solo show, makes its London debut, from the twenty-fourth of November to the twelfth of December.

Fly, You Fools! is a fringe-hit Lord of the Rings parody at Southwark Playhouse Borough, from the twenty-fifth of November to the sixteenth of January, with press night on Friday the twenty-seventh.

At the New Diorama, Orlando: A Pornobiography, a world premiere, runs until the twelfth of December. It has adult content.

Over at The Other Palace, Garry Starr: Classic Penguins runs to the twenty-ninth of November.

Further ahead, Parasocial Activity arrives at Southwark Playhouse Borough in January, and Jitters at Soho Theatre in February.

Films

Friday the twentieth of November brings The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping from Lionsgate. At the B F I Southbank, Filipinana opens the same day, with four-star reviews quoted, and the B F I adds more screenings of Ken Russell's The Devils, plus thirty-five millimetre screenings and a December programme. No confirmed release was found for Friday the twenty-seventh, though Focker-in-Law is dated Wednesday the twenty-fifth.

Food

The Bridge Social's DINE supper is pay what you can, plant-based, with mixed shared tables, from six to eight thirty p m on the first Thursday of the month. The next sitting is Thursday the third of December. Booking is due to go live on Eventbrite at six p m on the third Thursday, which is today, so it should already be open and will likely fill quickly.

Roobarbs, a plant-based South London outfit, has a festive Supper Club at Gipsy Hill Taproom and Four Boroughs in Crystal Palace, plus a Supper Night at Brucan Bakery. Dates and prices were not confirmed, and both venues are drinks venues, so check for food-only tickets.

On openings, hg, the Honest Greens restaurant, opens in Victoria on the twenty-fifth of November on Buckingham Palace Road. It is plant-forward but also serves meat, fish and feta, so check the menu for vegan options. Botanical Boys The Bistro at Coal Drops Yard was due on the eleventh, but its site still says opening soon.

Comedy

Robert White's extra show is at Top Secret, three p m on Sunday the twenty-second, for twenty pounds. Top Secret also releases one pound tickets for some shows, so check its listings for this week's under twenty pound slots.

Paul Foot: The Future is at Soho Theatre from the twenty-third of November to the fifth of December, at seven fifteen p m, for twenty-five pounds.

Diane Chorley plays Soho Theatre Walthamstow on Friday the eleventh of December, doors at eight p m, with tickets from twenty-six to forty-one pounds.

And Vir Das is at Soho Theatre on the twelfth, eighteenth and nineteenth of December. Only resale listings were found, from ninety-seven pounds, so check the theatre for face value.
